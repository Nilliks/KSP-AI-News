Announcer voice (ElevenLabs, optional, own key)

With an ElevenLabs key the announcer speaks. Without one it still speaks the stock lines that come with the mod, and everything else shows as captions under the video. ElevenLabs bills by the letter: a launch with big-moment calls is roughly 600 to 1,500 letters. The countdown numbers and other short stock lines are saved after the first time, so they're only paid for once.
